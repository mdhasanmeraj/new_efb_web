Privacy Policy. 
2. What Are Cookies 
Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make 
websites function, to improve user experience, and to provide information to website owners about how 
their site is used. 
3. Types of Cookies We Use 
Necessary Cookies 
These cookies are required for the Website to function properly, such as enabling core functionality and 
security. Without these cookies, parts of the Website may not work as intended. 
Analytics Cookies 
These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the Website, such as which pages are visited 
and how long visitors spend on the site. We use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager for this 
purpose. Google Tag Manager itself does not collect personal data but is used to manage and deploy 
other tags, including Google Analytics, on the Website. 
Marketing Cookies 
These cookies are used to measure the effectiveness of our marketing and to understand engagement 
with our advertising. We use Meta Pixel for this purpose. 
4. Third-Party Services We Use 
● Google Analytics — collects information about Website usage to help us understand visitor 
behavior 
● Google Tag Manager — manages and deploys tags (including analytics tags) on the Website 
● Meta Pixel — helps us measure and improve the performance of our marketing activities 
These third-party providers may process certain technical information (such as your IP address, browser 
type, and device information) on servers located outside the United Arab Emirates, as described further 
in our Privacy Policy. 
5. Managing Cookies 
A cookie consent banner or preference centre is not currently implemented on the Website. You can 
manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, 
delete, and block cookies, including cookies from specific websites. Please note that disabling certain
